The itinerary

Day by day

ColomboTrincomaleePasikudahJaffnaWilpattuAnuradhapura

Day 1Colombo → Trincomalee

01 / 10

Day 1ColomboTrincomalee

Sunset on the east coast

  • Travel to Trincomalee (about 6 hours by car).
  • Check in to a beachfront resort in Nilaveli or Uppuveli.
  • Romantic sunset walk on the beach.
  • Private candlelight dinner by the sea.
Day 2Trincomalee

Snorkelling at Pigeon Island

  • Morning: Leisurely breakfast by the beach.
  • Take a private boat to Pigeon Island for snorkeling among colorful corals.
  • Afternoon spa treatment for couples.
  • Evening: Watch sunset from Koneswaram Temple Cliff overlooking the ocean.
Day 3TrincomaleePasikudah

A villa on the bay

  • Drive to Pasikudah (~2.5 hours).
  • Check-in to a luxury beachfront resort with private villas.
  • Spend the day swimming and relaxing in calm shallow waters.
  • Romantic dinner on the beach or private deck.
Day 4Pasikudah

A beach picnic for two

  • Private boat ride along the coast.
  • Optional: Visit local fishing villages or explore nearby coral reefs.
  • Beach picnic for two.
  • Evening stargazing by the shore.
Day 5PasikudahJaffna

North to Jaffna

  • Drive to Jaffna (~5 hours) through scenic northern landscapes.
  • Check in to a boutique hotel.
  • Leisurely evening stroll around Jaffna Fort and quiet streets.
  • Try authentic Jaffna cuisine at a cozy local restaurant.

About Jaffna

Day 6Jaffna

Temples, islands and hot springs

  • Visit romantic spots like Nallur Kandaswamy Temple and Casuarina Beach
  • Explore ruins of Delft Island by private boat trip (optional).
  • Sunset at Keerimalai Hot Springs, famous for its rejuvenating waters.
  • Intimate dinner at your hotel or a rooftop cafe

About Jaffna

Day 7JaffnaWilpattu

Safari and a bonfire dinner

  • Transfer to Wilpattu (~5 hours).
  • Check in to a jungle lodge or luxury eco-resort.
  • Afternoon wildlife safari—spot elephants, leopards, and birds together.
  • Evening: Romantic bonfire dinner under the stars.

About JaffnaAbout Wilpattu

Day 8WilpattuAnuradhapura

The ancient city at dusk

  • Morning safari or relaxing morning at the lodge.
  • Drive to Anuradhapura (~2.5 hours).
  • Stay in a charming boutique hotel near the ancient city.
  • Evening stroll near Sri Maha Bodhi tree.
  • Dinner at a quiet garden restaurant.

About WilpattuAbout Anuradhapura

Day 9Anuradhapura

Ruins and Mihintale together

  • Explore the UNESCO-listed ruins together.
  • Visit the serene Mihintale hill, a peaceful and spiritual spot.
  • Return for a relaxing spa session or private pool time at your hotel.
  • Romantic farewell dinner with traditional music.

About Anuradhapura

Day 10AnuradhapuraColombo

Last photos, then home

  • Leisurely breakfast.
  • Transfer back to Colombo (~4.5 hours) or directly to Bandaranaike Airport.
  • Optional stop at a scenic spot for final couple photos.

About Anuradhapura

Journey highlights

  • Private beachside dinners & sunset walks in Trincomalee and Pasikudah
  • Snorkeling at Pigeon Island Marine Sanctuary
  • Boutique stays in Jaffna with authentic Northern cuisine
  • Wildlife safari and bonfire dinner in Wilpattu National Park
  • UNESCO heritage exploration in Anuradhapura
  • Personalized touches: private chauffeurs, romantic picnics & more

🌿 insightful tips

  • Best travel season: March to September (dry season in the North & East)
  • Book early for beachfront resorts with private villas
  • Pack light linen clothing, swimwear, and reef-safe sunscreen
  • Combine with a honeymoon package for added perks
  • Respect local customs in temples and heritage sites
