Thrill, Chill, and Explore: The Ultimate Active Adventure Across Sri Lanka

Sports-Based Tour

10 daysBespoke tourTailor-made

Home › Tours › Sports-Based Tour

Get ready for a high-energy adventure across Sri Lanka! This 10-day sports-based tour blends adrenaline and nature—from white-water rafting in Kitulgala and hiking the lush highlands of Ella, to surfing the world-class waves of Arugam Bay. Along the way, enjoy cycling, rock climbing, golf, and water sports, all guided by experts. Perfect for active travelers, this journey balances thrills with stunning scenery, cultural encounters, and plenty of time to unwind on the beach.

Itinerary

Day 1 · Arrival in Colombo

  • Arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport.
  • Rest and short city tour.
  • Optional: Visit a gym or sports club for light training.

Day 2 · Colombo to Negombo – Water Sports & Beach Activities

  • Head to Negombo (30-40 mins from Colombo).
  • Activities: Jet skiing, windsurfing, paddleboarding, and snorkeling.
  • Relax on Negombo beach.

Day 3 · Negombo to Kitulgala – White Water Rafting

  • Travel to Kitulgala (approx. 3.5 hrs).
  • Full-day white-water rafting on the Kelani River (Grade 2-3 rapids).
  • Optional: Trekking and canyoning nearby.

Day 4 · Kitulgala to Kandy – Hiking & Cultural Experience

  • Morning hike to scenic viewpoints near Kitulgala.
  • Travel to Kandy (approx. 2.5 hrs).
  • Evening visit to Kandy Lake and cultural show.

Day 5 · Kandy to Nuwara Eliya – Cycling & Nature Trekking

  • Early morning cycling tour around Kandy hills.
  • Travel to Nuwara Eliya (approx. 2.5 hrs).
  • Trekking in Horton Plains National Park, visit World’s End viewpoint.

Day 6 · Nuwara Eliya – Golf & Adventure

  • Play golf at Nuwara Eliya Golf Club (one of the oldest in Asia). Optional: Visit a tea plantation or take a nature walk.
  • Optional: Visit a tea plantation or take a nature walk.

Day 7 · Nuwara Eliya to Ella – Hiking & Rock Climbing

  • Travel to Ella (approx. 2 hrs).
  • Hike Ella Rock or Little Adam’s Peak.
  • Try rock climbing or rappelling.

Day 8 · Ella to Arugam Bay – Surfing

  • Long drive to Arugam Bay (approx. 5-6 hrs).
  • Relax on the beach and get ready for surfing
  • Evening surf session for beginners or intermediates.

Day 9 · Arugam Bay – Full Day Surfing & Beach Sports

  • Full day of surfing, beach volleyball, or kayaking.
  • Explore nearby lagoons for paddleboarding or bird watching.

Day 10 · Arugam Bay to Colombo – Departure

  • Return to Colombo (approx. 6 hrs).
  • Last-minute shopping or visit a sports shop.
  • Departure from Bandaranaike International Airport.

Journey highlights

  • Water Thrills in Negombo: Jet skiing, windsurfing, snorkeling, and beach sports.
  • White-Water Rush: Conquer the Kelani River rapids in Kitulgala (Grade 2–3).
  • Highland Adventures: Hike Horton Plains & Ella Rock, cycle the Kandy hills.
  • Tee Off in the Tea Country: Play at the iconic Nuwara Eliya Golf Club.
  • Climb & Trek: Try rock climbing, rappelling, and jungle trekking in Ella.
  • Surfing in Arugam Bay: Ride the waves at one of Asia’s top surf spots.
  • Full-Body Exploration: Cycling, kayaking, canyoning & nature immersion.

Insightful tips

  • Fitness Prep: Moderate fitness recommended for hiking, rafting & surfing.
  • Best Travel Season: March to September (ideal for Arugam Bay surfing).
  • What to Pack: Swimwear, activewear, hiking shoes, sunscreen, hydration gear.
  • Balance Adventure with Recovery: Spa and wellness stops can be added en route.
  • Try local fusion dining experiences in both Kandy and Galle for memorable meals
  • Private Transport: All intercity transfers included for comfort and flexibility.

FAQ

Is this tour suitable for beginners in water sports and hiking?

Yes! Most activities offer beginner-friendly options, with professional guides and instructors to assist.

Can I customize the activities based on my fitness level?

Absolutely—activities can be swapped or adjusted (e.g., leisure walks instead of treks, or yoga instead of rock climbing).

Are all gear and safety equipment included?

Yes, all safety gear for rafting, surfing, climbing, etc., is provided by certified operators.

Can families or teens join this tour?

Definitely. It’s ideal for sporty families with teens over 12 years. Some intensity levels can be adjusted.

Are there wellness or relaxation options included?

Yes—spa treatments, yoga sessions, and quiet nature stays can be woven in for recovery between high-adrenaline days.
