The Gini Raksha

Raksha masks are one of Sri Lanka's three traditional mask families, alongside Kolam and Sanni masks. They are carved from light kaduru wood, mostly in Ambalangoda on the south coast, and worn in processions and folk dance.

The Gini Raksha is the fire demon, crowned with flames. This one is our own drawing, made for this page.

See masks carved by hand at the Ambalangoda mask museum on our Cultural & Heritage Tour.
