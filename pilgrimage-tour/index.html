The trip

Embark on a soulful journey with Sunbird Lanka Tours, where travel becomes a path to spiritual awakening and inner peace. Our thoughtfully curated pilgrimages go beyond traditional sightseeing, guiding you through Sri Lanka’s sacred sites, ancient wisdom, and serene landscapes. Led by compassionate and knowledgeable local guides, each experience invites quiet reflection, meaningful connection, and personal transformation. From temple visits to time-honored rituals, discover a deeper sense of purpose in every step—supported by the heartfelt hospitality of those who call this island home.

Day 2ColomboAnuradhapura

Stupas and the sacred Bodhi tree

  • UNESCO World Heritage Site – Walk through one of the world’s oldest continuously inhabited cities.
  • Jetavanaramaya Stupa – Marvel at one of the tallest brick structures on Earth, believed to enshrine Buddha’s sacred waistband.
  • Abhayagiri Monastery – Discover ancient relics and the golden-leaf Tripitaka scriptures in a once-thriving centre of Buddhist learning.
  • Thuparamaya – Visit Sri Lanka’s first stupa, built to house the Buddha’s right collarbone.
  • Mirisavatiya Stupa – Stand before the resting place of King Dutugemunu’s sacred scepter, infused with royal devotion.
  • Sacred Bodhi Tree – Pay respects at the 2,300-year-old tree grown from the original Bodhi tree under which Buddha attained enlightenment.
  • A Day of Devotion – Immerse yourself in a living heritage of faith, architecture, and ancient wisdom.

🌿 insightful tips

  • Dress Modestly at Sacred Sites:Wear light, respectful clothing that covers shoulders and knees. White is often worn when visiting temples as a sign of purity and reverence.
  • Carry a Shawl or Sarong - Perfect for covering up quickly at religious sites, and useful against sun or chilly mountain breezes.
  • Footwear Etiquette: Always remove your shoes and hats before entering a temple. Bring socks or temple-friendly sandals for hot stone pathways.
  • Respect Rituals: Avoid turning your back to Buddha statues when taking photos. Watch or join rituals quietly and respectfully.
  • Hydrate & Prepare for Climbs: Carry water and light snacks, especially for treks like Adam’s Peak. A walking stick and flashlight will help during the early morning ascent.
  • Best Times for Visits:Early mornings are ideal for most sacred sites—cooler temperatures, fewer crowds, and a more peaceful atmosphere.
  • Temple Donations & Offerings While not mandatory, small donations or floral offerings are welcome. Always use your right hand or both hands to give or receive.

FAQ

What is the ideal time of year for this pilgrimage tour?

November to April offers the best weather—dry in the Cultural Triangle and cooler in the hill country. Adam’s Peak pilgrimage season typically runs from December to May (Full Moon to Vesak).

What should I wear to religious sites?

Dress modestly: Shoulders and knees should be covered Light, breathable clothing is ideal Carry a scarf or sarong for temples White attire is considered respectful at sacred sites

Is this tour physically demanding?

Mostly easy to moderate. However: Adam’s Peak climb involves over 5,000 steps and takes 3–5 hours Dambulla and Mihintale also have stairs Good general fitness and comfortable walking shoes are recommended.

Is the tour suitable for solo travellers or spiritual seekers?

Absolutely. The journey is ideal for: Solo travelers looking for meaning Couples seeking a mindful escape Groups exploring Buddhist heritage We also offer optional guided meditations and quiet reflection time.

Do I need to be Buddhist to join this tour?

No. The tour is open to all faiths and backgrounds. Respect and openness to spiritual traditions is all that’s required.

Can I participate in temple rituals or meditations?

Yes. Many sites offer public pujas (offerings) and meditation halls. Guided participation can be arranged where appropriate.
