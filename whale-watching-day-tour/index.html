Sail with Giants of the Deep!

Whale Watching Day Tour

1 dayWildlife & day tourTailor-made

Home › Tours › Whale Watching Day Tour

Set off from Sri Lanka’s southern coast—one of Asia’s top whale watching hotspots—for an unforgettable ocean adventure. Witness the awe-inspiring Blue Whale, the largest animal on Earth, and the magnificent Sperm Whale, as they glide through the waters near Mirissa. Just one moment of eye contact with these gentle giants is enough to leave you breathless. Elegant, powerful, and mysterious—this is marine magic at its finest.

Your adventure begins at 6:30 a.m. from the Mirissa Fisheries Harbor, where you’ll board a modern, fully-equipped vessel designed for both comfort and safety. With GPS, UHF radios, life jackets, and a trained crew certified in life-saving and first aid, you’re in expert hands.

Enjoy a freshly prepared breakfast on board, relax in comfortable seating, and even access clean Western-style toilets with shower facilities. The 4–5 hour cruise offers a 90–95% chance of spotting whales—so keep your eyes on the horizon!

After the tour, you can opt to extend your day with visits to Galle Fort, a Turtle Hatchery, or enjoy exciting water sports nearby.

Tour highlights – not to miss!

  • A magical moment of eye contact with majestic whales in the wild
  • Breathtaking views of the endless blue Indian Ocean

Things you'll experience

  • Get picked up from your hotel and transferred to the Mirissa Fisheries Harbor
  • Enjoy a freshly prepared breakfast on board the boat
  • Witness the incredible sight of Blue Whales and Sperm Whales in their natural habitat

FAQ

Is this day tour right for me?

Definitely! This adventure is perfect for anyone excited to journey into the ocean in search of whales. The boats are also accessible for wheelchairs and baby strollers, making it inclusive for all travellers.

Can the tour be customized?

Yes! We’re happy to tailor the experience to suit your needs. If you’d like to include additional stops or activities, just let us know in advance.

When is the best time to go?

Whale watching is possible year-round except in May and June due to rough monsoon seas. July to October: Trips depend on weather conditions. November to April: Ideal season with the highest chance of calm seas and successful sightings. If you're departing from Colombo, plan to leave by 4:00 a.m. to reach Mirissa by sunrise. If you're staying in Galle or along the southern coast, a 5:30–6:00 a.m. departure is perfect.

What do the transfers include?

Your tour includes hotel pickup, all attractions and activities, and a drop-off back at your hotel for a hassle-free experience.
