Uncover Sri Lanka’s Untouched Side – Culture, Coastlines & Charm

North & East Coast Tour

10 daysBespoke tourTailor-made

Home › Tours › North & East Coast Tour

Discover Sri Lanka’s hidden gems on this immersive 10-day journey through the island’s serene North and East. Explore the sacred ruins of Anuradhapura, experience the vibrant Tamil culture of Jaffna, and relax on the untouched beaches of Trincomalee and Pasikudah. From island temples and ancient forts to coral reefs and local cuisine, this tour blends heritage, nature, and coastal charm for a truly off-the-beaten-path adventure. Perfect for travelers seeking authenticity, tranquility, and unforgettable stories.

Itinerary

Day 1 · Arrival in Colombo → Transfer to Anuradhapura

  • Arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB)
  • Transfer to Anuradhapura Travel time: ~4–5 hours
  • Relax after your flight.
  • Optional: Explore Mihintale (cradle of Buddhism in Sri Lanka).

Day 2 · Explore Anuradhapura (UNESCO World Heritage Site)

  • Visit to Ruwanwelisaya
  • Sri Maha Bodhi Tree (oldest living tree in recorded history)
  • Abhayagiri Monastery, and Jetavanaramaya
  • Sunset at Isurumuniya Rock Temple.

Day 3 · Anuradhapura → Jaffna

  • Travel time: ~5 hours
  • En route: Stop at Thanthirimale Temple
  • Evening walk around Jaffna town & Jaffna Fort

Day 4 · Explore Jaffna & Northern Culture

  • Visit to Nallur Kandaswamy Temple
  • Dambakola Patuna
  • Point Pedro (northernmost tip)
  • Try Jaffna cuisine (crab curry is a must).

Day 5 · Nagadeepa & Delft Island Day Trip

  • Ferry to Nagadeepa Temple (Buddhist & Hindu shrines).
  • Continue to Delft Island for wild ponies, coral walls, and Dutch ruins.

Day 6 · Jaffna → Trincomalee

  • Travel time: ~5–6 hours
  • Check-in & relax on Uppuveli or Nilaveli Beach.
  • Evening: Optional whale watching (seasonal: May–Sept)

Day 7 · Explore Trincomalee

  • Visit to Koneswaram Temple
  • Lovers’ Leap
  • Fort Frederick
  • Snorkeling at Pigeon Island National Park

Day 8 · Trincomalee → Pasikudah

  • Travel time: ~2–3 hours
  • Relax on the calm, shallow Pasikudah Bay.
  • Enjoy water sports or a peaceful beach day.

Day 9 · Pasikudah → Sigiriya

  • Explore Polonnaruwa ancient ruins (UNESCO Site).
  • Continue to Habarana for overnight stay or optional elephant safari in Minneriya.

Day 10 · Sigiriya → Colombo or Airport

  • Early morning climb of Sigiriya Rock Fortress
  • Optional visit to Dambulla Cave Temples.
  • Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage (if route is adjusted)
  • Travel back to Colombo or Bandaranaike International Airport.

Journey highlights

  • Ancient Capitals: Explore UNESCO sites like Anuradhapura & Polonnaruwa
  • Off-the-Beaten-Path North: Discover the Tamil culture and temples of Jaffna
  • Marvel at artistry – cave frescoes, stupa architecture, and traditional mask-making
  • Island Adventures: Ferry to Nagadeepa & Delft Island with wild ponies and coral forts
  • Pristine Beaches: Unwind on the untouched shores of Trincomalee and Pasikudah
  • Wildlife Optional: Add safaris at Wilpattu or Minneriya for elephant sightings
  • Cultural Marvels: Visit Koneswaram Temple, Sigiriya Rock, and Dambulla Caves

Insightful tips

  • Best Season: May–September offers ideal weather in the North & East
  • Getting Around: A private car with driver is recommended for comfort and flexibility
  • Dress Code: Wear modest clothing for temple visits (cover shoulders and knees)
  • Foodie Tip: Try authentic Jaffna crab curry and Trinco seafood specialties
  • Camera Ready: Bring a wide-angle lens for landscapes and a zoom for wildlife/island shots

FAQ

Is this region safe for travellers?

Yes, North and East Sri Lanka are peaceful and welcoming, with improving infrastructure and warm local hospitality.

Can I swim and snorkel in Trincomalee and Pasikudah?

Absolutely! Both offer calm, shallow waters ideal for swimming, snorkelling, and water sports—especially between May and September.

Are there enough facilities for families or elderly travellers?

Yes. The itinerary can be adjusted for comfort, with quality hotels, private transport, and shorter travel legs when needed.

Can this itinerary be customized?

Definitely. You can extend beach stays, add wildlife safaris, or include more cultural interactions based on your interests.
