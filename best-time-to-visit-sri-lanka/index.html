Two monsoons, one island

There is always a sunny side

Sri Lanka has two monsoons, and they arrive at different times of year. From May to September the south-west monsoon brings rain to the west and south coasts and the hill country, while the east coast is dry and sunny. From about November to February the north-east monsoon rains on the north and east, and the south and west have their best weather. Turn the dial to your travel month to see where to go.
