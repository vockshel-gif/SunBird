Booking Terms & Conditions

Home › Booking Terms & Conditions

Welcome to Sunbird Lanka Tours, a proud Sri Lankan travel company dedicated to crafting unforgettable journeys across the island. These Booking Terms & Conditions outline the responsibilities of both Sunbird Tours and our valued guests. By confirming a booking, you agree to the following terms, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable travel experience.

1. Confirmation of booking

A 40% deposit is required at the time of booking to confirm your reservation.

Final payment: The remaining 60% of the total tour cost must be paid no later than 21 days prior to the start of the tour.

Payment methods:

  • International bank transfers (bank charges to be borne by the client)
  • Credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, AMEX – additional 3.5% transaction fee may apply)
  • PayPal (upon request)
  • Cash payments can be made locally for on-the-spot bookings or add-ons

Note:

  • All payments must be net of bank charges.
  • No hidden fees: our prices are transparent and inclusive.
  • Exchange rates are pegged to the Central Bank of Sri Lanka at the time of booking.
  • Currency: All prices and payments are quoted in US Dollars (USD) unless otherwise specified. Payments in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR) can be arranged based on the daily exchange rate.

Late bookings: For bookings made within 21 days of the tour start date, full payment is required at the time of booking.

2. Tour package inclusions

a) Accommodation: Hotel stays in shared double/twin rooms, inclusive of daily breakfast and dinner.

b) Transportation: Comfortable, private transport arranged according to group size:

  • 2 persons: Private car with a chauffeur driver
  • 4–7 persons: Private van with a chauffeur driver
  • 8–20 persons: Air-conditioned tourist coach with a national guide, driver, and assistant

3. Tour package exclusions

  • International and domestic airfare
  • Visa fees, and travel & health insurance
  • Lunch (available at additional cost or can be arranged independently)
  • Extra beds for children (children are expected to share existing beds with parents; extra beds are subject to availability and additional charges)
  • Entrance fees to sites, attractions, and activities (unless explicitly stated as included)
  • Personal expenses, including but not limited to laundry services, mini bar and in-room dining, alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages, telephone calls and other hotel incidentals

4. Cancellation & refund policy

a) Cancellation by traveller:

  • More than 30 days before tour start date: 80% refund of total amount paid
  • 15 to 30 days before tour start date: 50% refund of total amount paid
  • 14 days or less before tour start date: No refund

b) No shows or early departures: No refunds will be provided for unused portions of the tour or no-shows on the start date.

c) Cancellation by organizer: In the unlikely event of cancellation by the tour provider due to unforeseen circumstances (e.g., natural disaster, political unrest), a full refund will be issued, or an alternative travel date offered.

d) Refund processing time: Approved refunds will be processed within 14 business days to the original method of payment. Special cases (e.g., early check-outs, no-shows) are assessed individually.

COVID-19 & emergencies: We offer 100% refund if cancellation is due to pandemic-related restrictions within 21 days of arrival.

5. Travel safety & force majeure

We prioritize your safety. In case of political unrest, natural disasters, or travel bans, we ensure:

  • 90% refund (30+ days before travel)
  • All recoverable costs refunded (within 30 days or during travel)

6. Changes to itinerary

a) By Sunbird Lanka Tours: We aim to stick to your original itinerary. However, in unavoidable situations (e.g., hotel overbooking), changes will be discussed and fairly adjusted, including refunds for downgraded services or coverage of transfer costs.

b) By you: We offer flexible itinerary adjustments even after confirmation. Additional charges may apply, and we’ll inform you of any pricing differences in advance.

7. Special requests

Have dietary needs, room preferences, or mobility requirements? Let us know during booking and reconfirm via email. While we strive to fulfill every request, we cannot guarantee them unless explicitly confirmed in writing.

8. Travel insurance

Comprehensive travel insurance is mandatory for all travelers. You must disclose pre-existing conditions, pregnancy beyond 24 weeks, or age 70+ to ensure tour suitability.

9. Medical & age considerations

You are responsible for confirming medical readiness for the tour. Please consult your doctor and notify us of any limitations or medical conditions before booking.

10. Independent travel & external bookings

If your itinerary is only partially booked through Sunbird Tours, we are not responsible for third-party services such as flights, rail tickets, or hotels booked independently.

11. Complaints & customer care

Our team is committed to ensuring your trip runs smoothly. If issues arise:

  • Address them with the relevant service provider immediately.
  • Escalate unresolved matters via email to Sunbird Tours within 24 hours.
  • Failure to report issues promptly may affect eligibility for compensation.

12. Accommodation & hotel changes

Once hotel selections are finalized, changes are only permitted in unavoidable circumstances and subject to availability and additional cost. Please research selected accommodations beforehand. If a client insists on changes, any additional cost will be borne by the client.

13. Shopping disclaimer

We do not endorse or guarantee purchases made during the tour (e.g., jewelry, crafts, etc.). All purchases are at your discretion and risk.

14. Pricing guarantee

Your package price will not be affected by currency fluctuations. Only government-imposed changes may affect final costs.

15. Required travel documents

Guests are responsible for ensuring all personal travel documents (passport, visa, insurance, vaccination, etc.) are valid and in order before travel.

16. Legal responsibility

Sunbird Lanka Tours assumes liability only in cases of proven negligence for services we directly manage. Clients are encouraged to maintain personal safety and take reasonable precautions throughout the trip.

17. Disclaimer of information

While we strive for accuracy, travel content may rely on sources such as SLTDA, Wikipedia, Lonely Planet, TripAdvisor, and hotel websites. Temporary changes (e.g., renovations, weather) may impact experiences, and we are not liable for third-party inaccuracies.

For questions, contact our team at letstravel@sunbirdtours.lk. We’re here to make your journey safe, seamless, and unforgettable.
