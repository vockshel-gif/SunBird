Kalpitiya

Dolphins, Kites & Quiet Lagoons

Best Feb – Sep1 tour stops here

Plan a trip with KalpitiyaSee the tours

Kalpitiya is a long, narrow peninsula on the north-west coast, with the open ocean on one side and a sheltered lagoon on the other. Large pods of spinner dolphins feed offshore, steady winds have made the lagoon a favourite of kitesurfers, and small mangrove islands dot the water of Dutch Bay.

When to go

JFMAMJJASOND

BestMixedRainy

November to April for dolphin watching on calm seas; May to October for the strongest winds and the best kitesurfing.
