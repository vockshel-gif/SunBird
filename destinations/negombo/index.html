Negombo

Where Your Sri Lankan Journey Begins

Best Dec – Apr5 tours stop here

Plan a trip with NegomboSee the tours

Just minutes from the airport, Negombo is the perfect first or last stop in Sri Lanka. With golden beaches, a breezy coastal vibe, and rich colonial charm, it’s more than a transit town. Discover historic churches, canals from the Dutch era, and the scenic Negombo Lagoon—all in one laid-back seaside escape.

Where to eat

From beachside bites to global flavours, Negombo’s dining scene has it all. While hotels cater to every craving, standout spots include Mr. Burger, Tusker, Sea View, Port Beach Restaurant & Pub, Dolce Vita, Simply Strawberry, and the ever-popular Lords—serving everything from fresh seafood to international favourites.

Where to shop

While in Negombo, browse through charming local stops like Negombo Plaza, Cartier Jewels, Diana Souvenirs, and Tea Tone for Ceylon tea and keepsakes. For more extensive retail therapy, vibrant Colombo is just a short drive away.
