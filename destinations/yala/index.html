Yala

Where Wild Meets Ancient Wonder

Best Dec – Apr3 tours stop here

Plan a trip with YalaSee the tours

Yala National Park is Sri Lanka’s top wildlife destination, famous for having one of the world’s highest leopard densities. Here, jungles meet the coastline, offering thrilling safaris with sightings of elephants, sloth bears, crocs, and peacocks. Beyond the wild, Yala hides ancient secrets—centuries-old stupas and reservoirs whisper stories of forgotten kingdoms.

Where to eat

Dining in Yala blends luxury with wilderness charm. Many upscale hotels offer exceptional culinary experiences within their premises. For local flavors and casual dining, top picks include The Restaurant, The Chill, The Orchid, and Chef Lady Restaurant — each offering a unique taste of the region.

Where to shop

Yala is all about nature and adventure, so shopping options are minimal. Most hotels offer small boutiques with local handicrafts and souvenirs. For more variety, head to nearby towns for traditional items and artisan goods.
