Ella

Highlands, Hikes & Heavenly Views

Best Jan – Mar5 tours stop here

Plan a trip with EllaSee the tours

In the misty highland of Sri Lanka is Ella, a lush green village with jaw-dropping views all around. Its landscape is dotted with mountains, valleys, and features Sri Lanka’s most famous railway line. The waterfalls of Ella are the most beautiful. On a clear night, the distant glow of the Great Basses lighthouse on the south coast is visible from Ella. And, at times, you can even see the Uda Walawe National Park. Situated in the tea country, with a 1000m elevation, its tea plantations in Ella thrive all around. Fresh mountain air and the mild climate make Ella a quiet scenic retreat for any weary raveler. There are plenty of mountain treks for a lovely walking holiday.

Where to eat

Ella is packed with cozy cafés and laid-back eateries perfect for refueling after a day of adventure. Sip and dine at the stylish Ceylon Tea Factory, unwind at the ever-popular Café Chill or Dream Café, take in panoramic views at Ella 360, or enjoy homestyle Sri Lankan flavors at the charming Matey Hut—all fantastic treats for the hungry traveler.

Where to shop

Ella may be a quaint village, but it’s a great spot to pick up premium Ceylon tea straight from the source. While you won’t find big-name boutiques, the charm lies in its local tea outlets and small shops offering authentic, highland-grown blends—perfect souvenirs from Sri Lanka’s hill country.
