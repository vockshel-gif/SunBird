Mirissa

Waves, Whales & Coconut Dreams

Best Dec – Apr3 tours stop here

Plan a trip with MirissaSee the tours

Nestled on Sri Lanka’s southern coast, Mirissa is where thrill meets chill. Cruise out for epic whale and dolphin encounters, catch perfect surf breaks, or simply lounge on its crescent beach fringed with swaying palms. As the sun sets, Mirissa’s vibrant nightlife comes alive—making every moment here pure seaside magic.

Where to eat

From sizzling seafood to fusion bites, Mirissa’s dining scene is full of flavour. Top picks include Zephyr for beachside vibes, Margherita Italiano for authentic pizza, Little Tuna for sushi lovers, and Wood Space and Shady Lane for laid-back café charm. Don’t miss the local favourite Dewmini Roti Shop or sunset cocktails at Sunset Bar—each a perfect taste of Mirissa.

Where to shop

Mirissa may be laid-back, but it has its own style. Sip local coffee at Podi Kade while browsing handmade souvenirs, pick up dazzling Sri Lankan gems at Roosara, grab chic swimwear at Lazy Ride, or gear up with branded surf equipment from Dylan’s—perfect keepsakes from the coast.
