Nuwara Eliya

Little England in the Hills

Best Jan – Mar8 tours stop here

Plan a trip with Nuwara EliyaSee the tours

With misty mountains, endless tea plantations, and cool, crisp air, Nuwara Eliya feels like a highland escape from a bygone era. Once the favourite hill station of British colonials, it’s now a dreamy destination for scenic train rides, cascading waterfalls, tea tasting, boating, golfing, and invigorating hikes. At 2,000 m above sea level, this timeless retreat blends natural beauty with nostalgic charm.

Where to eat

From elegant hotel dining to cozy local favourites, Nuwara Eliya serves up a feast for every palate. Enjoy authentic flavours at Grand Indian and Grand Thai, savour Italian at Salmiya, or try the inviting dishes at Queenswood and Indian Summer. Whether you’re after comfort food or a fine dining experience, the city’s culinary scene won’t disappoint.

Where to shop

Nuwara Eliya offers a charming mix of shopping experiences. Browse woollens at the famous Bale Bazaar, pick up homemade preserves at the Franciscan Sister’s Jam Room, or explore the Grand Hotel Shopping Gallery for unique finds. Don’t miss the tea collections at Mackwoods Tea Centre or the sweet indulgences at the Strawberry Farm. For a touch of local craftsmanship, stop by the Jessica Factory.
