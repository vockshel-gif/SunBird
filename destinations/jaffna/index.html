Tucked in Sri Lanka’s highlands, Hatton is a dreamy escape wrapped in emerald tea fields, misty mornings, and colonial charm. From the spiritual climb to Adam’s Peak to scenic strolls by Castlereigh Reservoir, every view is postcard-perfect. With iconic estates like Bogawantalawa and Maskeliya, this is the heart of Ceylon tea country—serene, scenic, and steeped in legacy.

Where to eat

Jaffna’s food scene is a flavorful celebration of its rich Tamil heritage. Popular spots like Mangos, Malayan Café, Cosy Restaurant, Green Grass, Jaffna Heritage Hotel, and Hotel Rolex serve up local favourites—from spicy curries to dosas—making them must-visits for any culinary explorer.

Where to shop

While Jaffna may not have trendy boutiques, its vibrant markets and street vendors offer an authentic shopping experience. Browse for juicy Jaffna mangoes, traditional sweets, unique palmyra crafts, and an array of fresh local produce—true tastes of the north.
