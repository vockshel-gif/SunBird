The tour

The trip

At Sunbird Lanka Tours, your journey deserves more than just a place to stay — it deserves an experience. We handpick exceptional accommodations that seamlessly blend world-class comfort with local character. Whether you crave a beachfront paradise, a boutique mountain retreat, or an eco-luxury hideaway, every stay reflects the true spirit of Sri Lanka.

Originally established to protect displaced wildlife during the creation of the Uda Walawe Reservoir, this stunning park became a designated National Park in 1972. Spanning over 31,000 hectares of diverse landscapes—from grasslands to forests—it is now a thriving sanctuary for elephants, birds, and countless other species. A favorite among both tourists and locals, Udawalawe offers a truly immersive wildlife experience in the heart of Sri Lanka.

A heartwarming stop on your journey, the Elephant Transit Home cares for orphaned elephant calves until they’re strong enough to return to the wild. Watch as these playful young elephants are bottle-fed every three hours—a daily routine that continues year-round. This sanctuary offers a rare glimpse into wildlife conservation in action and a chance to witness the gentle side of Sri Lanka’s giants.

Tour highlights – not to miss!

  • Witness majestic Sri Lankan elephants in their natural habitat
  • Take in the views of one of the country’s largest reservoirs
  • Enjoy the heartwarming sight of baby elephants being bottle-fed at the Elephant Transit Home

Things you'll experience

  • Embark on an exciting Udawalawe Jeep Safari
  • Savor authentic Sri Lankan Rice & Curry in a welcoming local home
  • Interact with elephants at the Elephant Transit Home
  • Enjoy peaceful moments of bird watching amidst nature

FAQ

Is this day tour right for me?

Absolutely! If you’re fascinated by wildlife and love nature documentaries, the Udawalawe Jeep Safari will be a thrilling, real-life adventure you won’t forget.

Can the tour be customized?

Yes! We’re happy to tailor the experience to suit your preferences. Just let us know in advance if you’d like to include any specific stops or experiences not listed in the itinerary.

When is the best time to go?

We recommend departing from Colombo at 7:00 a.m. for a full-day adventure, returning later that same evening.

What do the transfers include?

Our tour includes hotel pickup, all listed attractions, and a drop-off back at your hotel for your convenience and comfort.
