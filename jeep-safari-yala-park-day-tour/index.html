Where Leopards & Giants Rule the Wild

Yala Jeep Safari Day Tour

1 dayDay tourTailor-made

Home › Tours › Safari & wildlife › Yala Jeep Safari Day Tour

Step into the untamed wilderness of Yala National Park, Sri Lanka’s most visited and wildlife-rich reserve. Spanning over 130,000 hectares, Yala is famous for its high leopard density, making it one of the best places in the world to spot these elusive big cats. Located 260 km southeast of Colombo, the park boasts an incredible mix of forests, scrublands, lagoons, and grasslands, teeming with wildlife. From majestic elephants and sloth bears to colorful peacocks and stealthy crocodiles, Yala offers a safari adventure like no other. Recognized by Forbes as a must-visit destination, this day tour is your chance to witness Sri Lanka’s wild side—raw, real, and unforgettable. Book your Yala Jeep Safari today and explore where nature thrives and predators prowl!

Declared a National Park in 1938, Yala was once a favored hunting ground of British elites—today, it stands as a protected haven for wildlife. Spread across five blocks, with two open to visitors, the park is a vibrant stage of nature’s drama. On your jeep safari, expect thrilling sightings—from mischievous monkeys leaping through treetops to dazzling peacocks and the park’s most iconic resident: the stealthy Sri Lankan leopard, silently weaving through the wilderness.

Tour highlights – not to miss!

  • Spot Sri Lankan leopards and majestic elephants in their natural habitat
  • Enjoy bird watching in one of the island’s richest ecosystems
  • Take a serene beach walk along Yala’s golden sandy shores

Things you'll experience

  • Embark on an exhilarating jeep safari through Yala National Park
  • Observe rare and exotic wildlife in their natural surroundings
  • Take in stunning landscapes—from dense jungles to pristine beaches, rivers, and lakes
  • Enjoy a hearty Sri Lankan meal at a welcoming local home

FAQ

Is this day tour right for me?

Absolutely! Whether you're a first-time wildlife explorer or a passionate animal lover, this tour offers awe-inspiring moments and unforgettable encounters with nature.

Your experience includes hotel pickup, visits to all tour attractions, and a safe drop-off back at your hotel all arranged for your convenience.
