Leopards, elephants and whales

Sri Lanka safari & wildlife tours

7 toursPrivate and tailor-made

Home › Tours › Safari & wildlife

Sri Lanka is one of the best places in Asia to see a leopard, and wild elephants roam many of its national parks. Our jeep safaris run as day trips to Yala, Wilpattu and Udawalawe, and from December to April you can sail out from Mirissa to look for blue whales.

Want more than a day? The longer tours below add safaris to a full trip around the island, and the Bird Watching Tour follows Sri Lanka’s birds from misty cloud forests to coastal lagoons.
