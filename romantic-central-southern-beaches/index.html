The trip

Escape to the heart of paradise with a 10-day romantic journey through Sri Lanka’s misty hills and sun-kissed southern shores. From tranquil lakeside strolls in Kandy to cozy colonial nights in Nuwara Eliya and intimate beachfront dinners in Mirissa, this tailor-made couple’s escape blends culture, nature, and seaside serenity—perfect for honeymoons, anniversaries, or just reconnecting in style.

Day 1ColomboKandy

Dinner by the lake

  • Transfer to Kandy (~3.5 hours).
  • Evening: Visit the Temple of the Tooth and enjoy a cultural dance show.
  • Relax after your flight.
  • Romantic dinner by the lake.

About Kandy

Day 2Kandy

Gardens and tea tasting

  • Morning walk in Peradeniya Botanical Gardens.
  • Explore Kandy town — quaint cafes, craft shops.
  • Afternoon tea tasting experience at a local tea house.
  • Leisure evening stroll around Kandy Lake.

About Kandy

Day 3KandyNuwara Eliya

“Little England”

  • Scenic drive through misty tea plantations (~2.5 hours).
  • Visit a tea factory en route.
  • Stay at a colonial-style hotel with cozy fireplaces.
  • Romantic evening walk around Gregory Lake

About KandyAbout Nuwara Eliya

Day 4Nuwara Eliya

Waterfalls and a couple’s spa

  • Visit nearby waterfalls like Lover’s Leap or St. Clair’s Falls.
  • Horseback riding or boating on Gregory Lake.
  • Enjoy a cozy couple’s spa treatment in the afternoon.
  • Dinner at a quaint restaurant with mountain views.

About Nuwara Eliya

Day 5Nuwara EliyaElla

Sunset over Ella Gap

  • Short scenic drive (~2 hours).
  • Check into a luxury resort with panoramic views of Ella Gap.
  • Afternoon hike to Little Adam’s Peak or visit the Nine Arches Bridge.
  • Romantic sunset dinner overlooking the valley.

About Nuwara EliyaAbout Ella

Day 6EllaMirissa

Candlelight on the beach

  • Transfer to Mirissa (~5 hours).
  • Check into a beachside villa or luxury resort.
  • Relax with a sunset beach walk and swim.
  • Private beach candlelight dinner.

About EllaAbout Mirissa

Day 7Mirissa

Whales and slow afternoons

  • Morning whale watching cruise (seasonal: Nov–Apr).
  • Afternoon relaxation on the beach or spa treatment.
  • Evening stroll through Mirissa town, enjoy fresh seafood.

About Mirissa

Day 8MirissaUnawatuna

Galle Fort and the sea

  • Short drive to Unawatuna or Galle (~30–45 minutes).
  • Explore the Galle Fort, a UNESCO World Heritage site.
  • Beach relaxation or snorkeling at Unawatuna.
  • Romantic dinner in a historic fort hotel or beachfront restaurant.

About Mirissa

Day 9Unawatuna

A day of your own

  • Day at leisure on Unawatuna beach or visit nearby Jungle Beach for privacy.
  • Optional cooking class for couples or a yoga session.
  • Sunset drinks at a rooftop bar with ocean views.
Day 10UnawatunaColombo

Breakfast by the beach, then home

  • Leisurely morning, breakfast by the beach.
  • Transfer to Colombo (~2.5 hours) or Bandaranaike International Airport.

Journey highlights

  • Temple of the Tooth visit with a romantic lakefront dinner in Kandy
  • Stroll through Peradeniya Botanical Gardens hand-in-hand
  • Cozy up in colonial charm in Nuwara Eliya’s misty highlands
  • Visit waterfalls like Lover’s Leap & enjoy couple's spa time
  • Scenic hike to Little Adam’s Peak & view the iconic Nine Arches Bridge
  • Beachfront candlelight dinner in Mirissa
  • Whale watching cruise for two (Nov–Apr)
  • Wander through the romantic lanes of Galle Fort
  • Couple’s yoga, spa rituals & private dinners by the ocean

Insightful tips

  • Best Time to Travel: December to April for ideal beach & whale watching conditions
  • Pack smart: Include light jackets for the hill country and beachwear for the coast
  • Surprise your partner with a private picnic or a flower bath in the hills
  • Upgrade to the observation car on the train to Nanu Oya for panoramic romance
  • Try local fusion dining experiences in both Kandy and Galle for memorable meals
  • Don’t miss golden hour photos at Nine Arches Bridge or Galle’s ramparts

FAQ

Is this tour suitable for honeymooners?

Absolutely! This itinerary is designed with romance in mind—luxury stays, intimate dinners, and couple-friendly experiences.

Can the activities be customized for anniversaries or proposals?

Yes! We can arrange romantic surprises like private dinners, flower arrangements, or even proposal planning assistance.

What’s the average travel time between destinations?

Most transfers are 2–5 hours by private car, with scenic routes and optional stopovers to make the journey pleasant.

Can we skip whale watching if we visit off-season?

Of course! You can swap it for a spa session, a beach day, or a private boat cruise instead.

Are luxury or boutique hotel upgrades available?

Yes. From cliffside villas to heritage mansions, we offer fully customizable accommodation options.
