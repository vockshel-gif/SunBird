The trip

Embark on a captivating 10-day journey through Sri Lanka’s living history, where every sunrise brings you face-to-face with ancient capitals, sacred temples and UNESCO treasures. Wander beneath the world’s oldest Bodhi tree in Anuradhapura, climb the legendary Lion Rock of Sigiriya and lose yourself in the frescoed caves of Dambulla. Experience the rhythmic pulse of Kandyan dance in mist-clad Kandy, sample fragrant high-grown teas on a scenic hill-country rail ride to Nuwara Eliya, then stroll the ramparts of Dutch-era Galle Fort as the Indian Ocean breeze carries stories of traders past. Along the way, discover local crafts—from intricate mask-making in Ambalangoda to batik and pottery workshops—and savor Sri Lanka’s vibrant cuisine, from fiery hoppers to sweet Kandy delights. This is more than a tour—it’s a soul-stirring immersion in a land where heritage lives on in every smile, every ritual, and every stone.

Day 1Colombo

A first taste of Colombo

  • Arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB)
  • Gangaramaya Temple – mix of modern architecture and ancient tradition
  • National Museum – introduction to Sri Lanka’s heritage
  • Evening: Cultural dinner in a colonial-era mansion
Day 2ColomboAnuradhapura

The sacred city

  • Drive to Anuradhapura (approx. 4 hrs)
  • Sri Maha Bodhi Tree (oldest living tree in recorded history)
  • Ruwanwelisaya Stupa, Abhayagiri Monastery, and Jetavanaramaya
  • Evening: Attend a local puja (offering) ceremony

About Anuradhapura

Day 3Mihintale

Where Buddhism began

  • Morning hike up to Mihintale, cradle of Buddhism in Sri Lanka
  • Visit Isurumuniya Rock Temple
  • Cultural activity: Learn about Buddhist rituals from a monk
  • Optional: Visit local village for a traditional lunch and rural lifestyle experience
Day 4AnuradhapuraSigiriya

Lion Rock and frescoes

  • Drive to Sigiriya
  • Climb the Sigiriya Rock Fortress – UNESCO World Heritage site
  • Explore ancient frescoes, mirror wall, and royal gardens
  • Afternoon visit to Dambulla Cave Temple (Golden Temple of Dambulla)
  • Evening: Cultural dance performance at the hotel

About AnuradhapuraAbout Sigiriya

Day 5Polonnaruwa

The medieval capital

  • Full-day tour of Polonnaruwa – medieval capital
  • Gal Vihara, Parakrama Samudraya, Royal Palace ruins
  • Optional: Rent bikes to explore the vast ancient city
  • Return to Sigiriya

About Polonnaruwa

Day 6SigiriyaKandy

The hill capital

  • Stop at Matale Spice Garden en route
  • Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic
  • Walk around Kandy Lake
  • Evening: Attend a Kandyan dance performance with drumming and fire-walking

About SigiriyaAbout Kandy

Day 7KandyNuwara Eliya

Tea country

  • Scenic train ride or drive to Nuwara Eliya
  • Tea plantation and factory – learn about Sri Lankan tea heritage
  • Explore colonial-era buildings and the Grand Hotel
  • Evening: Traditional high tea experience

About KandyAbout Nuwara Eliya

Galle Fort at sunset

  • Drive to Galle (approx. 5–6 hrs with breaks)
  • Galle Dutch Fort – UNESCO site with colonial charm
  • Walk the ramparts, visit churches, and explore art galleries
  • Evening: Sunset over the Indian Ocean from the Fort

About Nuwara EliyaAbout Galle

Masks and local artisans

About Galle

Journey highlights

  • Step into history at sacred cities like Anuradhapura, Polonnaruwa & Sigiriya
  • Witness living Buddhism – meditate at Mihintale, join a puja under the Bodhi Tree
  • Marvel at artistry – cave frescoes, stupa architecture, and traditional mask-making
  • Feel the rhythm of culture – Kandyan dance, drumming & fire-walking shows
  • Travel in style – ride the scenic highland train through tea country
  • Stroll colonial lanes in Galle Fort and enjoy high tea in Nuwara Eliya
  • Create your own art – optional batik, pottery, and village lunch experiences

Insightful tips

  • Dress respectfully for temples (shoulders & knees covered)
  • Pack comfy footwear – expect climbing, cobbled streets, and temple grounds
  • Try local cuisine – rice & curry, hoppers, and a must-try: Kandy sweet meats
  • Souvenir ideas: Handloom batik, wooden masks, Ceylon tea & moonstones

FAQ

Is this a spiritual or religious tour?

It blends cultural history with living traditions – ideal for those curious about Buddhism, heritage, and local life.

How physically demanding is the itinerary?

Moderate – expect some stairs (Sigiriya, Mihintale), optional hikes, and long drives between regions.

Are workshops and village visits included or optional?

They’re optional add-ons to let you choose your level of hands-on experience.

Can this itinerary be adapted for senior travellers?

Yes – it can be adjusted for comfort, including vehicle-based sightseeing and slower pacing.
