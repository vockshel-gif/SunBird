The trip

This 7-day wellness journey is designed for those seeking deep relaxation, healing, and spiritual balance. From serene beaches to misty hill retreats, immerse yourself in Ayurveda, yoga, and the natural rhythms of island life. A perfect reset for the mind, body, and soul.

Journey highlights

  • Authentic Ayurvedic treatments guided by certified practitioners.
  • Daily yoga & meditation in serene, natural environments.
  • Organic meals, herbal teas, and Ayurvedic cuisine.
  • Guided forest bathing and mindful hikes in the hill country.
  • Tranquil beach time to relax and recharge.

Insightful tips

  • Best time to travel: November–April (West/South Coasts), May–August (East Coast).
  • Wear light cotton clothing suitable for tropical wellness environments.
  • Avoid alcohol and heavy meals during the program for best results.
  • Communicate any health conditions beforehand for personalized care.
  • Bring a journal—many guests find inspiration and insight during their journey.

FAQ

Is prior yoga or meditation experience required?

Not at all! Our instructors welcome all levels and adjust practices to suit your needs.

Can I continue my treatments after the tour?

Yes. We offer follow-up plans and herbal products for home use.

Are meals included and vegetarian?

Yes, all meals are included and are mostly vegetarian or Ayurvedic. Vegan and gluten-free options are available.

Will I have free time?

Absolutely. Every day includes personal downtime to relax or explore your surroundings.
